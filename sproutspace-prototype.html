SproutSpace Prototype

  • Figma

An interactive mobile prototype designed to make shared plant care simpler, more engaging and accessible. The concept combines collaborative household management, task scheduling and gamified feedback to encourage consistent plant-care habits.

SproutSpace applies human–computer interaction principles such as intuitive navigation, visual consistency, clear feedback, and reduced cognitive load to create an accessible and engaging plant-care experience for users of different ages and technical abilities.

Role: UX/UI Designer and Researcher

Features: Shared household responsibilities, care reminders, task completion feedback, room-based organization and interactive plant status indicators
